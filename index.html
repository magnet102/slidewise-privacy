Slidewise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Slidewise: Number Block Puzzle ("the game") is made by Horus Games. This page explains what the game does with your data. Short version: we do not collect your name, email or any account. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics.

What is stored

  • Your progress (levels reached, stars, best moves, Daily Puzzle streak) and your settings (music, sounds, vibration, tutorial seen).
  • They are saved only in the game's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include these files in your own device backup (handled by Google under your Google account, not by us).

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The game shows optional reward videos (for extra hints or a solution) and, after level 10, a short ad between some levels. Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • To show and measure ads, AdMob collects device data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, app interactions with ads, and crash/diagnostic data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game first shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The Huawei version and phones without Google Play services show no ads.

Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

  • The game sends anonymous events (level started/finished, moves, hints used, ads watched), device model, OS version and a random install ID to GameAnalytics, to find levels that are too hard and fix bugs. See GameAnalytics privacy policy.
  • No name, email, contacts, photos or location are sent.

Sharing

The "Share" button after the Daily Puzzle opens Android's share menu with a short text (puzzle number, stars, moves and the store link). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

Permissions

INTERNET and network state (ads and statistics), VIBRATE (the short buzz when you move a piece; you can turn it off in Settings), and AD_ID (added by Google's ads SDK).

Children

The game is meant for ages 13 and up. Ads are limited to general-audience (PG) content.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Slidewise → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your progress and settings from the phone. To ask GameAnalytics or us to delete statistics linked to your install ID, email us.

Changes

If a future version uses data in a new way (for example cloud save), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date will change.
